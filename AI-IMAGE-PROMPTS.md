## Shared art direction

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: B2B cosmetic ingredient catalog product image. Photorealistic premium cosmetic-laboratory photography on a seamless warm ivory surface and softly curved warm-white backdrop, centered square catalog composition, generous negative space, soft diffused daylight, realistic material texture, clear and amber laboratory glass. No people, no finished cosmetic packaging, no brand, no readable labels, no text, no watermark, no medical symbols or molecular diagrams.
