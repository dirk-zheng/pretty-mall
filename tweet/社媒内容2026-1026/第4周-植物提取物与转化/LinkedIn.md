# LinkedIn｜A botanical extract is more than a plant name

“Green tea extract” or “Centella extract” is not a complete purchasing specification.

Before comparing suppliers, confirm:

- plant species and plant part;
- extraction solvent and ratio;
- marker compound or assay;
- carrier and preservative system;
- physical form, color and odor;
- pesticide, heavy-metal and microbiological limits;
- origin, traceability and available declarations.

These details influence color, odor, solubility, stability and how the material behaves in the finished formula.

China’s botanical ingredient supply chain offers multiple manufacturing and grade options, but meaningful comparison requires a consistent brief and grade-specific evidence.
