# LinkedIn｜From a buyer brief to China’s cosmetic ingredient supply chain

Effective sourcing does not begin with asking every factory for a price.

It begins by translating the buyer’s request into a specification that suppliers can answer consistently.

Our review sequence is:

1. Clarify identity: name, INCI, CAS and whether the material is a single substance or blend.
2. Define the grade: assay, carrier, physical form, origin and critical limits.
3. Confirm application: formula type, process, pH, use level and destination market.
4. Request evidence: specification, TDS, SDS, COA and relevant declarations.
5. Compare samples and commercial conditions against the same brief.

This makes communication with manufacturers in China more efficient and reduces the risk of comparing materials that share a name but not a specification.

We support the matching and review process; final approval remains subject to supplier documentation, sample testing and the buyer’s quality and regulatory assessment.
