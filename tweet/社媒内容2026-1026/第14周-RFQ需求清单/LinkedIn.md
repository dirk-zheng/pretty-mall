# LinkedIn｜A better cosmetic-ingredient RFQ starts with eight fields

You do not need to find the material in our catalog first. Start with what you actually need:

1. ingredient name and INCI;
2. CAS or other identity reference, if known;
3. exact grade, assay or key limits;
4. physical form and application;
5. required documents or declarations;
6. sample quantity and expected order quantity;
7. destination market;
8. target timing and packaging constraints.

If some fields are unknown, describe the formula goal and current reference material. We can use the brief to contact relevant partners across China’s cosmetic-ingredient manufacturing and supply chain, then return with questions, documents and sample options.
