# LinkedIn｜Same INCI does not always mean the same material

Two supplier samples can carry the same INCI name and still behave differently in a formula.

The difference may come from:

- assay or active content;
- carrier or solvent;
- molecular weight or particle size;
- extraction and processing method;
- origin and natural variation;
- stabilizer or preservative system;
- impurity and microbiological limits.

That is why supplier confirmation should go beyond “cosmetic grade.” A useful review compares the formal specification, TDS, SDS, composition statement, recent COA and a traceable sample from the proposed manufacturing source.

The final question is not only “Is this the same ingredient?” It is also “Is this specific grade suitable for the customer’s formula, process and target market?”
