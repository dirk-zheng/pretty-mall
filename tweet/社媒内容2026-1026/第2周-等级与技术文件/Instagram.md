## 页面文字

1. `Same INCI does not always mean the same grade.`
2. `Check assay and active content.`
3. `Check carrier, solvent and preservatives.`
4. `Check particle size, molecular weight or extraction.`
5. `Compare the formal specification and recent COA.`
6. `Test the actual sample in the intended formula.`

## Caption

Names help identify a material. Specifications help determine whether the grade fits the formula.

Before supplier approval, connect the INCI to the composition, formal specification, TDS, SDS, recent COA and a traceable sample.
