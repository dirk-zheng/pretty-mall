An ingredient can meet its release specification and still be put at risk by unsuitable packaging or storage.

Before ordering, confirm inner-contact material, closure and tamper evidence, pack size, light or moisture sensitivity, storage temperature, shelf life, retest policy and transport constraints. For hygroscopic powders, oxidation-sensitive oils or temperature-sensitive actives, these details deserve explicit agreement.
