# LinkedIn｜A good sample does not complete supplier qualification

Supplier review should connect the commercial offer to a controlled manufacturing source.

Useful questions include:

- Who is the actual manufacturer?
- Which site and line make the proposed grade?
- What quality system and batch-release controls apply?
- Which documents are current and grade-specific?
- How are raw-material, process, site and specification changes communicated?

We use these questions when coordinating with partners in China’s cosmetic-ingredient supply chain. The objective is not a generic promise; it is a clearer chain of evidence for the material being considered.
