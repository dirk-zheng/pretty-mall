# LinkedIn｜A niacinamide quote should include more than assay

Two offers can show the same headline purity and still differ in relevant details.

Ask for the test method, related-substance limits, moisture, residual solvents where applicable, microbiological limits, manufacturing information and a batch-specific COA. Then confirm that the document set fits the destination market and your quality system.
