A laboratory sample is useful only when its identity and acceptance criteria can be carried forward.

Record the supplier, manufacturer, grade, lot number, COA, sample date and test results. Confirm whether the commercial order will come from the same site and specification. Define which attributes require incoming verification and how deviations or substitutions will be handled.

When coordinating with China-based ingredient partners, we treat sample approval as a documented checkpoint—not an informal promise that every later batch will be identical.
