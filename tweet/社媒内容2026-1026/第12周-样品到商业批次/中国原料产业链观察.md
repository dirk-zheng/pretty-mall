### An approved sample is a checkpoint, not the end of sourcing

When an ingredient sample is coordinated through China’s supply chain, the sample can arrive before every commercial detail is fixed.

The next questions are operational:

- Will the commercial order come from the same manufacturer, site and grade?
- Does the approved sample have a lot number and matching COA?
- Will commercial packaging differ from the sample packaging?
- Are MOQ and lead time based on stock, a production run or repacking?
- How will substitutions or specification changes be communicated?

These checks help purchasing, R&D and quality teams understand what exactly moves from sample approval into the first commercial order.

The goal is not to promise that every batch will be identical. It is to create a traceable handover and define what must be checked again.
