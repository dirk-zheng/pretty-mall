A cosmetic ingredient catalog can only show a small part of the market.

Buyers may be looking for a specific INCI, CAS number, assay, carrier, origin, physical form or document package. That requirement may not appear in a featured catalog, but it can still be reviewed against relevant options in China’s cosmetic ingredient manufacturing and supply chain.

At Aurelia Pretty, we start with the sourcing brief:

- ingredient or trade name;
- INCI and CAS number, if known;
- requested grade, assay or composition;
- formula application and destination market;
- sample and technical-document needs;
- estimated quantity and delivery destination.

We then help investigate potential suppliers, grades, samples and available documents. Availability and suitability are confirmed only after reviewing the specific supplier, specification and batch information.
