### A large document pack is not the same as a confirmed grade

In China’s cosmetic-ingredient supply chain, documents may pass through a manufacturer, distributor and export contact before reaching the buyer.

The pack can look complete and still contain files referring to different grades, sites or revision dates.

Instead of counting documents, we try to connect them:

- Does the specification name the exact offered grade?
- Do the TDS, SDS and declarations identify the same manufacturer or source?
- Is the COA linked to the sample or batch being discussed?
- Are revision dates and test methods consistent?
- Is it clear who will communicate future changes?

This does not mean an intermediary is a problem. It means document ownership and product identity should remain clear across the supply chain.

## 建议素材

- 首选：真实但脱敏的文件关系图，只展示文件类型、等级代号、版本日期和关联状态。
- 可选：桌面实拍的核对清单，遮盖公司名称、证书号和完整批号。
- 不用 AI 文件截图冒充供应商资料。

## 表述边界

- 不声称经销或出口环节必然造成文件错误。
- 不以文件数量判断供应商质量。
- 仅展示获得授权且完成脱敏的资料片段。
