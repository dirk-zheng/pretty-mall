# Supplier Document Review Checklist

## Identity and version
- Manufacturer, site and exact grade are identified.
- Document name, revision date and version are visible.
- Trade name, INCI and composition information are consistent.

## Specification and batch evidence
- Formal specification separates limits from typical values.
- Test methods and units are stated where relevant.
- The COA belongs to the supplied sample or proposed batch.
- Critical impurity, microbiological and physical limits are reviewed.

## Safety and declarations
- SDS matches the exact material and current revision.
- Storage, handling and transport information is consistent.
- Required declarations are available.

## Commercial continuity
- Manufacturing site and grade are linked to the quotation.
- Change-notification expectations are documented.
- Shelf life, retest policy, pack format and MOQ are confirmed.

This checklist organizes a review; it does not replace regulatory, safety or formula assessment.
